Part 1

Tipe Rig Pemboran Rotary

Offshore

  • Bottom supported: Swamp barge, Tender assist, Conventional fixed platform, Jack-up (complaint tower)
  • Floater: Tension leg platform, Semi-submersible, Drillship

Onshore

  • Conventional — investasi besar, komponen berat, drilling floor luas.
  • Fast Moving — Truck mount, Semi-trailer.
  • Mobile

Conventional vs Mobile Rig

Conventional: tripping lebih cepat (stand di triple), tenaga lebih besar (drawworks & mud pump >1000 HP), hook load >200 ton, tapi investasi & hari pindah lebih besar.

Mobile: investasi awal lebih kecil, pindah lebih cepat, truk lebih sedikit, tapi tripping lebih lambat (stand di double) dan lebih banyak rig mekanikal (noise tinggi).

Part 3

Drilling System & Rig Components

a. Hoisting & Rotating System

  • Mast & Substructure — struktur menahan beban drill string; Derrick (fixed), Mast (portable, tipe Cantilever/Telescopic, tinggi single/double/triple joint)
  • Crown Block — puli tetap di puncak mast
  • Traveling Block — puli bergerak, digantung wire rope
  • Top Drive / Swivel — memutar drill string sekaligus jadi penghubung sirkulasi
  • Rotary Table — memutar drill string dari dasar (sistem konvensional)
  • Drawworks — drum penggulung wire rope (single/double drum)
  • Drilling Line & Deadline Anchor — wire rope hoisting & jangkar ujung mati

b. Mud Circulating System

  • Mud Tanks — Shaker tank, Intermediate tank, Active tank, Reserve tank
  • Mud Mixing Hopper
  • Triplex Mud Pump
  • Mud Recycle Equipment — Shale shaker, Desander & Desilter, Degasser
  • Surface High Pressure Line — Standpipe, Manifold & Rotary Hose

c. Power Generation & Control System

Mencakup generator, sistem kontrol drive (DC-DC, DC Drive/SCR, AC Drive/VFD) yang menyalurkan tenaga ke drawworks, mud pump, dan rotary system.

d. Well Control System

Rangkaian peralatan Blow Out Preventer (BOP) & pendukungnya untuk mengendalikan tekanan sumur, mencegah kick berkembang jadi blowout — mengacu pada API RP 53 & API Spec 16A/16C/16D.

Termasuk BOP stack (annular & ram preventer), choke manifold, kill & choke line, dan accumulator unit.

e. Drilling Instrumentation

  • Weight Indicator
  • Rate of Penetration (ROP)
  • Rotary RPM & Rotary Torque
  • Pump Stroke (SPM)
  • Pump / Standpipe Pressure
  • Casing Pressure

f. Drilling String & BHA

  • Drill Pipe
  • Bottom Hole Assembly (BHA): Heavy Weight Drill Pipe (HWDP), Drill Collar (DC)
  • Stabilizer
  • Bit Sub & Cross Over (X/O)

g. Tubular Handling Tools

  • Manual Tongs & Power Tongs / Iron Roughneck
  • Link & Elevator: Center latch elevator, Side door elevator, Single joint elevator
  • Slips: Drill pipe slips, Drill collar slips, Casing slips
  • Safety Clamp

Part 4

Rig Inspection & Maintenance

4 Kategori Inspeksi

  • Category I — observasi kinerja saat operasi
  • Category II — I + cek korosi, deformasi, komponen longgar/hilang, deteriorasi, pelumasan, retak eksternal
  • Category III — II + NDT area kritis, mungkin ada disassembly
  • Category IV — III + disassembly menyeluruh untuk NDT semua komponen primary-load-carrying

3 Tingkat Rekomendasi Temuan

  • Critical — berpotensi hilang nyawa, cedera serius, atau kerusakan lingkungan
  • Major — berpotensi merusak peralatan penting/mengganggu operasi
  • Minor — berpotensi memicu insiden atau standar operasi tidak terpenuhi
